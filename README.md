### IBM Rising Champions Program

>[Rising Champions](https://www.ibm.com/community/ibm-rising-champions/)

*An extension of the Champions program, Rising Champions is a graduated badging program, dedicated to recognizing and supporting IBM technology advocates year-round. Rising Champions demonstrate a commitment to advocacy, while imparting their knowledge and expertise to the technical community. The program also provides the primary pathway to become a Champion.*

*As a year-round program, you have the time and space to progress through the badging levels. To apply for the program, you need to complete an initial application, and then continue to report your activities to us on a regular basis. By adding activities, you can progress to higher badge levels, as you work towards the Champions nomination season.*

<img src="https://www.ibm.com/community/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Infographic-for-IBM-Rising-Champions.png" alt="IBM Rising Champions Infographic" height="500" width="700"/>

**Benefits:**
- **Graduated Badging System**
  - *Contributor*: Submit at least 2 acts of advocacy to earn your first badge.
  - *Advocate*: Complete a total of 5 acts (or 3 additional after Contributor).
  - *Influencer*: Reach 8 acts (or 3 additional after Advocate).

- **Year-Round Participation**
  - Report advocacy activities any time during the year to progress through badges and qualify for IBM Champion nomination.

- **Recognition Pathway to IBM Champion**
  - Gain visibility and credibility as an advocate, building your path toward becoming an IBM Champion.

- **Encouraged Advocacy Activities**
  - Participate in and report activities such as community posts, user group events, presentations, content creation, and product feedback.

- **Perks & Professional Benefits**
  - Invitations, discounts, and perks for IBM conferences and events.
  - Discounts and vouchers for IBM Learning and certifications (especially for Influencers).
  - Earn IBM-verified digital credentials via Credly.
  - Enhanced visibility, networking opportunities, and ongoing support.

- **Supported Advocacy Community**
  - Join regular community calls to enhance advocacy skills.
  - Connect with peers and IBM teams for collaboration and support.

- **Streamlined Start via Product Reviews**
  - Complete two product reviews (e.g., Gartner Peer Insights, G2, TrustRadius) to quickly earn the Contributor badge.

- **Open Eligibility**
  - Open to IBM clients, partners, and non-IBM advocates.
  - Previous advocacy activities in related IBM programs may count toward qualification.


#### Advocacy Credly Badges

*The following badges can be earned for participation in the Rising Champions Advocacy Program*

<img src="https://higherlogicdownload.s3.amazonaws.com/IMWUC/UploadedImages/dCCkTDtBRyuwYUV3wVLb_Screenshot%202023-12-21%20at%203.25.54%E2%80%AFPM-L.png" alt="Rising Champions Badges" heigh="500" width="700"/>

**How to Earn:**
- **Contributor Badge**
  - Submit at least 2 advocacy activities, such as community posts, presentations, or product reviews.
  - Earn your first IBM Rising Champions badge and gain initial recognition in the community.
  - Fast-track option: Complete two product reviews on platforms like Gartner Peer Insights, G2, or TrustRadius.

- **Advocate Badge**
  - Complete a total of 5 advocacy activities (or 3 additional after earning Contributor).
  - Demonstrate consistent engagement and dedication to supporting the IBM community.
  - Access to additional perks and opportunities within the program.

- **Influencer Badge**
  - Achieve a total of 8 advocacy activities (or 3 additional after earning Advocate).
  - Be recognized as a top advocate with significant impact and visibility.
  - Receive exclusive benefits, such as special event invitations, professional development opportunities, and discounts on IBM certifications.
